Our history

HOW IT ALL
STARTED

In 2019, five friends started meeting every Sunday at a court in the park. No rules, no schedule — just a ball and the will to play. By the end of summer two more teams had shown up, and it was clear: we needed a league.

The first season ran across three courts and brought together 6 teams. Referees were volunteers, stats were tracked in Google Sheets, and the trophy was welded by one founder's neighbor. It was a little rough around the edges — but it was real.

Today we have 18 teams, our own stats-tracking system, three partner gyms, and a referee crew. But the spirit stayed the same: basketball for everyone, no attitude, no unnecessary barriers.

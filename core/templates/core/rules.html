RULES

01
Section 01

General provisions

These rules govern the participation of teams and players in the PLAYON city league. By registering a team, the captain and all players automatically agree to the terms set out in this document.

1.1 The league runs annually from September to May, with a summer streetball season from June to August.
1.2 Any team that has registered on the website and meets the age requirements of the chosen category can join the league.
1.3 The organizer reserves the right to make changes to the schedule, the referee crew, and the tournament format, with at least 7 days' prior notice.
Important: participation in the league is voluntary and free for the base season. Tournaments with a prize fund may require an entry fee - details are listed in each tournament's announcement.
02
Section 02

Team registration

Teams are created through the personal account on the website. The user who registers the team automatically becomes its captain.

2.1 The team name must be unique within the chosen age category - duplicate names are not allowed.
2.2 The minimum roster for tournament participation is 5 players for basketball 5×5 and 3 players for streetball 3×3 (not counting substitutes).
2.3 The captain is responsible for the accuracy of all players' data, including age and contact information.
2.4 Changing the team captain is only possible by contacting the league administration.
Warning: submitting false information about a player's age may result in the team's disqualification from the tournament, with no refund of the entry fee.

05
Section 05

Fair Play and discipline

PLAYON is a league built on respect for opponents, referees, and spectators. Violating this principle is penalized regardless of the sporting outcome.

5.1 Insults, threats, or physical aggression toward referees, players, or spectators result in the player's immediate disqualification from the match.
5.2 Three technical fouls in one season result in the player automatically missing the next match.
5.3 The referee's decision on the court is final. Appeals are only possible through the process described in Section 07.
5.4 The team captain is responsible for the conduct of their players and the spectators who came to support them.
Zero tolerance: the use of physical force outside of play results in the player's disqualification from all league competitions for at least one full season.
06
Section 06

Tournaments and playoffs

The regular season determines team rankings for playoff qualification. The playoff format depends on the number of registered teams in the category.

6.1 Teams that finish at the top of the regular season standings advance to the playoffs.
6.2 The playoff format is single-elimination bracket.
6.3 The winner receives the season trophy and medals for the entire registered roster.
6.4 Registration for individual tournaments (cups, open events) happens through the Calendar page and does not depend on participation in the regular season.
07
Section 07

Appeals and disputes

If a team disagrees with a referee's decision or a disciplinary penalty, it has the right to file an appeal within 48 hours after the match.

7.1 The appeal is submitted by the team captain in writing to the league's official email address.
7.2 The league committee decides on the appeal within 5 business days.
7.3 The committee's decision is final and cannot be further appealed.
